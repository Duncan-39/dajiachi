How it works

From group chat to table in three steps

  1. 1

    Start a poll

    Start a poll and share the code or link with your group.

  2. 2

    Everyone answers

    Each person answers three quick questions on their own phone. Nobody has to speak up in the chat about what they can’t eat.

  3. 3

    Get your shortlist

    Da Jia Chi searches near a fair meeting point, ranks places by who has to travel furthest, and tells you what you still need to confirm with the restaurant.
